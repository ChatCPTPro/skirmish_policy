Skirmish Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

In short: Skirmish has no adverts and doesn't track you. We don't ask for your name, location, contacts or photos. Playing in a web browser needs a sign-in by email; the app doesn't. Beyond that, the game keeps only what it needs to play: the moves in your game and any short chat messages you type to the other player.

Who we are

Skirmish is a two-player board game for iPhone and iPad, made by Colin Truran in the United Kingdom. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email skirmishsupport@chatcpt.pro.

What the game stores

  • The game itself. When you create or join a game, the board, the moves and the score are kept on our game server so both players see the same game and can come back to it.
  • Chat messages. Messages you type in a game's chat are stored with that game so the other player can read them. Each message is at most 100 characters, and only the last 10 messages in a game are kept. Older ones are deleted automatically. Typed chat is only on when both players have agreed to it; otherwise players choose from a set of ready-made phrases. Games against the computer use the ready-made phrases only.
  • Games against the computer. When you play a computer opponent, we keep a record of the moves so we can make the computer play better. This record has no chat and nothing that identifies you. Your chat messages are never read by the computer opponent.
  • On your device. The app saves the code of the game you're in, so you can rejoin it after closing the app. This stays on your device.

Players appear in the game only as "Arthur" and "Lancelot". There are no usernames or profiles.

Keeping chat safe

Every message you type is checked before the other player sees it. First our own server looks for bad language and for personal details such as phone numbers, email addresses and social media names. Then the text of the message is sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which checks it for content such as harassment, hate, sexual content, threats and self-harm. Only the text of that one message is sent — never your email address, your name, the game code or anything else that identifies you. OpenAI doesn't use it to train its models and doesn't keep it for its own monitoring. Ready-made phrases aren't sent anywhere.

If a message is blocked, the other player never sees it and it is not stored, with one exception: if it is blocked as sexual content involving children or as a sign that someone may harm themselves, we keep that message with the sign-in email of the player who sent it for 30 days, so we can act on it, and then delete it.

Each blocked message counts against the player who sent it. We keep the times of these (never the messages) for 90 days. Three in one game turn typed chat off for that player for the rest of the game; if that happens in three games, typed chat stays off for that player until we have reviewed it.

If the checking service can't be reached, typed chat pauses for a short time and the ready-made phrases keep working. The game itself never depends on chat.

Report and block. You can report the other player from the chat panel. A report keeps the last 10 messages of that game with both players' sign-in emails, for 30 days, so we can review it; it then is deleted. You can also block the other player: you won't be seated together again, and chat between you closes at once. We keep the list of players you've blocked.

Signing in (web browser only)

To play Skirmish in a web browser at skirmish.chatcpt.click you sign in with your email address: Cloudflare Access emails you a one-time code. We keep your email address, when you signed in, and which version of the Terms of Use you agreed to, so that we can let you back in and know that you agreed. Your email address is not shown to other players and isn't used for anything else. The iPhone and iPad app doesn't currently ask you to sign in.

What we don't collect

Apart from the sign-in email described above, we don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. The game has no advertising and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It keeps anonymous usage figures for each game (such as its length and number of moves) to plan capacity and costs; they contain nothing that identifies you. Cloudflare also measures page speed and visits without cookies. We don't sell your data, and we share it with no one except the services listed below, for the purposes described.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs our game server. Like any website, it sees the network (IP) address your device connects from, which it uses to deliver and protect the service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Apple TestFlight. While Skirmish is in testing, it's installed through Apple's TestFlight app. Apple may share basic tester information with us, such as how many times the app was installed, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI checks the text of typed chat messages for harmful content, as described under "Keeping chat safe". It receives the text only, doesn't use it to train its models and doesn't keep it. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Children

Skirmish is made for players of all ages. Because the game has no accounts and asks for no personal information, children can play without sharing any. Chat starts with ready-made phrases; typed chat needs both players' agreement and is checked (see "Keeping chat safe"), but we still suggest children don't put personal details such as their name or school in chat messages. Parents who have any concern can contact us at the address above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Tell us the email address you signed in with, or the game code (shown in the app), and we'll delete what we hold — your sign-in record, or that game and its chat. Email skirmishsupport@chatcpt.pro. This includes any chat report or blocked message we are keeping under "Keeping chat safe".

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
