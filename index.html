Skirmish Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

In short: Skirmish has no accounts and no adverts, and it doesn't track you. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. The only things the game keeps are what it needs to play: the moves in your game and any short chat messages you type to the other player.

Who we are

Skirmish is a two-player board game for iPhone and iPad, made by Colin Truran in the United Kingdom. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email skirmishsupport@chatcpt.pro.

What the game stores

  • The game itself. When you create or join a game, the board, the moves and the score are kept on our game server so both players see the same game and can come back to it.
  • Chat messages. Messages you type in a game's chat are stored with that game so the other player can read them. Each message is at most 100 characters, and only the last 10 messages in a game are kept. Older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Games against the computer. When you play a computer opponent, we keep a record of the moves so we can make the computer play better. This record has no chat and nothing that identifies you. Your chat messages are never read by the computer opponent.
  • On your device. The app saves the code of the game you're in, so you can rejoin it after closing the app. This stays on your device.

Players appear in the game only as "Arthur" and "Lancelot". There are no usernames or profiles.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. The app has no advertising and no analytics, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites. We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs our game server. Like any website, it sees the network (IP) address your device connects from, which it uses to deliver and protect the service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Apple TestFlight. While Skirmish is in testing, it's installed through Apple's TestFlight app. Apple may share basic tester information with us, such as how many times the app was installed, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Skirmish is made for players of all ages. Because the game has no accounts and asks for no personal information, children can play without sharing any. Chat is typed freely, so we suggest children don't put personal details such as their name or school in chat messages. Parents who have any concern can contact us at the address above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we don't know who players are, tell us the game code (shown in the app) and we'll delete that game and its chat. Email skirmishsupport@chatcpt.pro.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
